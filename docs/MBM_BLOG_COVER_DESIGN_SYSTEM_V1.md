# MBM Blog Cover Design System v1

Status: Approved working design direction, 11 October 2026. Every individual cover requires editorial review and approval before publication.

## Purpose
Explain a specific B2B business problem visually. Premium, modern, editorial, commercially literate. Do not reuse the same illustration for unrelated topics.

## Visual identity
Use existing MBM brand guidelines as the source of truth. Palette: Ink #111015, Plum #2C1834, Ivory #F4F0E9, Lime #D6FF45 (restrained accent), Mauve #BDB5BD (supporting metadata). Do not adopt navy/orange from the inspirational mockup as an unapproved rebrand. Keep approved wordmark unchanged.

## Composition
- Master cover: 1200 x 630 px (approximately 1.91:1 for social sharing), with an optional 16:9 website crop. Keep key content within a safe central area.
- One concept-led illustration or meaningful diagram: e.g. lead qualification funnel for lead-to-opportunity article.
- Strong legible editorial headline where needed. Ensure headline remains readable on mobile and small thumbnails; avoid overcrowding with text duplicated from the web page.
- Clear hierarchy, restrained typography, high contrast, accessible alt text, no generic stock photos or invented data.
- When showing numbers or outcomes, use verified data or label hypothetical examples plainly.

## Production and approvals
1. Define reader problem, central idea and illustration rationale.
2. Create an on-brand cover concept and suitable site/social crops.
3. Review for factual accuracy, typography, image relevance, accessibility and branding.
4. Obtain Vikash's approval of the specific asset before website publication.
5. Record article URL, image filename, version and approved status.

## Reference
Approved design direction takes inspiration from the previously discussed premium editorial B2B funnel concept and page composition, not a literal reusable mockup. Individual covers remain subject to review.
